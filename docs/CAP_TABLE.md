## 9. Tax & Jurisdiction Notes (Canada)

| Topic | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Lifetime Capital Gains Exemption (LCGE)** | ~$971K (2024, indexed) on qualified small business corporation (QSBC) shares. Requires 24-month hold, 90%+ active business assets. **Critical to qualify.** |
| **SR&ED Credits** | Eligible for ~15–35% refundable tax credit on qualifying R&D salaries/contractors. File annually. |
| **QSBS (US)** | Not applicable unless US C-corp. If US investors require flip, do it *before* value inflects. |
| **Founder Tax** | Share purchase @ nominal = no immediate tax. Vesting = no tax until disposition (if 83(b) equivalent filed). |
| **Employee Options** | Taxed at exercise (not grant) if CCPC. Deferral available. |
