Campus — Pricing

School management software, running entirely on your own server.
One license. No per-student fees. Ever.
That's not a starting price — it's the whole model. Most school-management software charges more every time your enrollment grows. Campus doesn't: the price is for the software, not for how many students or staff use it.

Base package — $1,450

Everything Campus does — registration, scheduling, attendance, grades & report cards (with GPA / grading schemes), billing with optional online payments, communications, booking, IEPs, and the parent/student portal — installed on one server. No networking beyond that machine. Includes install support and 30 days of post-install support. No student or user limit, now or ever.

Online payments run through your school's own Stripe, Paystack or Flutterwave merchant account: parents pay on the gateway's hosted page, the money goes straight to your account, and Campus never sees or stores card details. The gateway's own transaction fees are charged by the gateway. Where no supported gateway serves your country, payments are recorded manually.

Networking

LAN access for multiple devices (front desk + classroom machines on the same network)+$250
Remote access — Cloudflare Tunnel, WireGuard VPN, or your own domain (plain gateway)+$500

Your data is never stored anywhere but your own server, in every mode — turning remote access on only changes how a connection is routed to reach it, never where anything lives. The three remote options differ solely in how much a third party can see a connection while it's in transit; pick based on your own privacy comfort, not price — all three cost the same.
